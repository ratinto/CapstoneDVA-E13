================================================================================
DATA COLLECTION METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Primary Source:        LAPD Incident Reporting System
Collection Method:     Official police reports filed by patrol officers
Data Entry System:     LAPD Records Management System (RMS)
Quality Assurance:     Multi-level review by supervisors and data analysts
Geocoding System:      Automated address-to-coordinate conversion
Privacy Protection:    PII removed; addresses rounded to 100-block level

Crime Classification:  Uniform Crime Reporting (UCR) standards
Reporting Standard:    FBI National Incident-Based Reporting System (NIBRS)
Time Recording:        24-hour format, rounded to nearest minute

================================================================================
DATA QUALITY NOTES
================================================================================

Completeness:
  - Victim demographics: 90-95% complete
  - Geographic coordinates: 98% complete
  - Weapon descriptions: 70% complete (not applicable for many crimes)
  - Premise descriptions: 95% complete

Accuracy:
  - Addresses rounded to nearest 100 block for privacy
  - Times recorded to nearest minute
  - Crime codes standardized to UCR definitions
  - Coordinates verified through LAPD geocoding system

Known Limitations:
  - Delayed reporting may affect Date Reported vs Date Occurred
  - Some crime types may be reclassified during investigation
  - Missing data in optional fields (e.g., weapon for non-violent crimes)
  - Historic records may have different data entry standards
